Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

MyOrbitDrive is free during preview, so there is nothing to refund or cancel today. This page explains how refunds will work if paid plans are introduced.

Cancelling

Free use can be stopped at any time by removing your devices in the Windows app. A paid plan, if offered, will be a one-time pass for a fixed period that does not renew by itself, so there is nothing to cancel.

Refunds for paid plans

  • Within 7 days of payment: a full refund if the plan was not activated, you were charged more than once, or it does not work as described and we cannot fix it within 3 working days of your report.
  • Within 24 hours of payment: a refund if you change your mind and have not used any paid feature.
  • No refund once a plan has been used and worked as described, or after it has ended.

How to ask

Email support@myorbitdrive.app within 7 days of payment with your order number and account email. We reply within 2 working days. Approved refunds go back to the original payment method, usually within 7 to 10 working days; your bank or wallet may take longer to show it.
