Delivery & Service Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

MyOrbitDrive sells only digital services. Nothing is posted or shipped, and there are no delivery charges.

Free services

The web app and the Windows app preview are available right after you sign in and install. The Free plan limits (3 files per day, up to 10 MB each) apply from the date announced on the Services page. You pair your own devices and choose your own folders.

Paid plans (Plus and Office)

A paid plan is switched on once payment is confirmed, normally within 24 hours when a payment needs a manual check. The period starts when the plan is activated and its end date will show in your account. You will see an on-screen confirmation.

Support

If a payment succeeded but the plan is not active after 24 hours, or anything does not work, contact us through the Contact page with your order number. We will either activate it or refund it under the Refund Policy.
