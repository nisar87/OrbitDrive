Services

Last updated: 24 November 2026

MyOrbitDrive is a digital service: a Windows app and a web app that let you open folders you choose on your own PC from your phone or any browser. Nothing physical is sold or shipped. During the early version every feature below is free; the plans and limits below start on the date announced on this page.

1. Web file explorer

Browse the shared folders of your PC in any browser, with sorting, filtering and list or icon views.

2. Folder sharing with approval

Share only the folders you tick. Read-only by default, and every new folder is approved on the PC.

3. Device pairing

Pair a phone or browser with a one-time code or QR, approved on the PC. Remove a device at any time.

4. File preview

Open pictures and PDFs in a preview before you download them.

5. Large downloads

Files are streamed, not loaded into memory, and a download can resume after an interruption.

6. Download as ZIP

Select several files or a folder and download them as one ZIP archive .

7. Send to my phone

Right-click a file in Windows and send it to your phone's Inbox in the web app.

8. Windows app

A small app on your PC where you choose folders, see paired devices and see activity. The current build is an unsigned early version.

Plans and prices

Prices are in US dollars, with the Pakistani rupee (PKR) amount beside them. Each paid plan is a one-time pass for a fixed period and does not renew by itself. Buying the same plan again adds time after your current pass; buying a higher plan starts now.

The Office plan covers more PCs and devices for one organisation: the owner invites people and everyone shares the plan’s limits. The owner and admins manage people and see counts only, never file or folder names, and never what a member moved.

What “unlimited” means

Where a plan says unlimited it means no limit on that item (Office has no limit on devices or on the number of files). The size and daily data limits in the table always apply; nothing is slowed quietly.

A file counts when a download or a send to your phone finishes; failed or cancelled transfers do not count. Thumbnails and browsing folders do not count. Opening a file to view it counts towards your daily data, and the largest-file limit applies to it. The daily count resets at midnight, Pakistan time.

Early version status: MyOrbitDrive is an early version, and paid plans and the free limits above are not switched on yet. They start on the date announced on this page. Until then no payment is taken, ordering is not offered and every service is free.

Your PC must be on, awake and running the app for its files to be reachable.
