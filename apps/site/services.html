Services

Last updated: 24 November 2026

MyOrbitDrive is a digital service: a Windows app and a web app that let you open folders you choose on your own PC from your phone or any browser. Nothing physical is sold or shipped. During the preview every feature below is free; the plans and limits below start on the date announced on this page.

1. Web file explorer

Browse the shared folders of your PC in any browser, with sorting, filtering and list or icon views.

2. Folder sharing with approval

Share only the folders you tick. Read-only by default, and every new folder is approved on the PC.

3. Device pairing

Pair a phone or browser with a one-time code or QR, approved on the PC. Remove a device at any time.

4. File preview

Open pictures and PDFs in a preview before you download them.

5. Large downloads

Files are streamed, not loaded into memory, and a download can resume after an interruption.

6. Download as ZIP

Select several files or a folder and download them as one ZIP archive .

7. Send to my phone

Right-click a file in Windows and send it to your phone's Inbox in the web app.

8. Windows app

A small app on your PC where you choose folders, see paired devices and see activity. The current build is an unsigned preview.

Fair use

Unlimited means unlimited for ordinary business use. If an account moves far more data than usual, we may contact you or slow it to protect the service for everyone.

A file counts when a download or a send to your phone finishes; failed or cancelled transfers do not count. Browsing folders and previews do not count. The daily count resets at midnight, Pakistan time.
