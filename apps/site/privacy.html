Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

MyOrbitDrive is operated by Nisar Ahmad (the "operator"), Fateh Jang, Attock, Punjab, Pakistan. This page explains what information the service handles and why.

Your files

Your files stay on your PC. MyOrbitDrive does not keep a permanent copy in a cloud drive. When you open or download a file from the web app, it travels from your PC to your browser through a relay server that passes it on. Every connection uses HTTPS, so the data is encrypted on its way. The relay does not store file contents or write them to logs. Because the relay ends each HTTPS connection in order to pass the file along, the service is not end-to-end encrypted.

Information we handle

This is the full list of what the relay (the server that connects your browser and your PC) holds about you, how long, and why.

  • Account sign-in: you sign in with an identity provider (Firebase Authentication). That provider holds your sign-in details such as your email address and password; the relay receives the identifier it needs to recognise your account.
  • Your email address and name: each time you sign in, the relay saves the email address (and the display name, if your sign-in gives one) from your verified sign-in with your account record, so the person who runs the service can see which account an action affects, for example before suspending it or checking a payment. Only the operator sees it. It is never shown to other customers, never to your organisation's owner or admins, never in a web address and never in the logs. It is removed when you delete your account. Daily backups of the relay's records, kept for seven days, may still hold it until they are replaced.
  • Devices and PCs: the names you gave your PCs and the browsers or phones you paired, when each was last seen, and which browser may reach which PC. Kept until you remove them or delete your account. The relay does not hold which folders you share or any folder or file names; those stay on your PC.
  • Your choices: how long to keep finished transfers in the list, and whether to show a message when a PC sends you a file.
  • Daily counts: how many files and how many bytes your account moved each day, kept for 40 days, so plan limits can be applied and you can see your own use.
  • Your plan, passes and orders: which plan you are on and when it ends. If you order a pass: the plan, length, amount, discount code, the payment method you used, the payment reference you give us, the dates, and the operator's decision. See Payments below.
  • Organisations: if you join one, the organisation name, the label you chose, your role, and a log of who joined, left or was changed. Its owner and admins see counts only, never file or folder names.
  • Operator records: when the operator suspends an account, signs browsers out, changes prices or settings, or decides an order, a record is kept with the account id and the email address the account had at that moment, the time and the reason, so the record stays readable. These records are kept without an end date, so the email address in them stays after an account is deleted; ask us to remove it if you want that.
  • Send to my phone: while a file offer is open the relay holds an identifier, the file's size and when the offer ends. It does not keep the file name: the name is sealed by your PC and is only shown after your PC opens it, so an offer made while your PC is off shows no name. Offers are removed after they end.
  • Files in transit: the relay passes a file through while you download it. It does not store the contents or write them to logs. While a download is open, the relay keeps the file's name in memory for up to one hour so the download can be named; that is not written to disk.
  • Logs: the relay's logs name the kind of request (for example "download started") and its result. They contain no addresses of files, no file names and no file contents.
  • Support messages: if you contact us, we keep your message and email address to reply.

Where it is handled: the relay runs on a server in the United Arab Emirates. Sign-in is provided by Firebase (Google); its data location is Qatar and Google's network. The service is not intended for children under 13.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your information.
  • We do not use advertising or tracking cookies, and this website loads nothing from third parties.
  • We do not read your files.

Payments

No payments are taken at present; paid plans are not switched on yet, and ordering is not offered in the app until the start date announced on the Services page. When it starts, you can order a pass and pay by bank transfer or mobile wallet; the operator checks the payment by hand. We do not receive or store card numbers or wallet logins. We store your order, the amount, the method you used and the payment reference you give us, as described above. Paid orders are kept after an account is deleted, reduced to the order, plan, length, amounts, method, reference and dates, because they are payment records we may need for tax and disputes.

Your choices

You can remove a device or stop sharing a folder at any time in the Windows app. You can download a copy of what the service holds about you, and delete your account, in the web app under Settings (both work even before you pair a PC, after a recent sign-in). Deleting disconnects every PC, removes your browsers and settings from the relay and deletes your sign-in record; it never deletes anything on your PCs. You can also email support@myorbitdrive.app; we reply within two working days.
