Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

MyOrbitDrive is operated by Nisar Ahmad (the "operator"), Fateh Jang, Attock, Punjab, Pakistan. This page explains what information the service handles and why.

Your files

Your files stay on your PC. MyOrbitDrive does not keep a permanent copy in a cloud drive. When you open or download a file from the web app, it travels from your PC to your browser through a relay server that passes it on. The relay does not store file contents or write them to logs. The relay can technically see traffic while it is in transit, because the service is not end-to-end encrypted.

Information we handle

  • Account sign-in: you sign in with an identity provider (Firebase Authentication). That provider holds your sign-in details such as your email address; we receive the identifier needed to recognise your account.
  • Device and pairing information: names and identifiers of your PC and the devices you pair, and which folders you chose to share.
  • Activity records: events such as pairing, approvals and transfers shown in the app, so you can see what happened. Records describe actions; they do not contain file contents.
  • Support messages: if you contact us, we keep your message and email address to reply.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your information.
  • We do not use advertising or tracking cookies, and this website loads nothing from third parties.
  • We do not read your files.

Payments

No payments are taken at present; paid plans are not switched on yet. When paid plans start, card or wallet details will be handled by the payment provider and not stored by us, and this policy will be updated first.

Your choices

You can remove a device or stop sharing a folder at any time in the Windows app. To ask for your account information to be deleted, email support@myorbitdrive.app. We reply within two working days.

Changes and contact

We will post changes on this page and update the date above. Questions: Contact.
