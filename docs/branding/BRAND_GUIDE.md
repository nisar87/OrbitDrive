# MyOrbitDrive brand guide (PROVISIONAL, Phase B and C foundation)

Status: **provisional direction, not approved, not published.** Nothing in this folder is used by the product or any deployed site. Replacing the app's icons, favicon or name needs the owner's approval.

## 1. Concepts compared
Rendered side by side in `CONCEPT_BOARD.png` (large, 96/48/32/16 px, greyscale, light, dark, mono on teal, stacked, on a tinted background). Reproduce with `python3 docs/branding/tools/logos.py docs/branding/concepts && python3 docs/branding/tools/board.py`.

| Concept | Idea | Small sizes | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A Connected M** | An M whose centre vertex is a connection node | Still reads as an M at 16 px; node survives at 32 px | **Provisional pick.** Simple, distinctive enough with the node, carries the "My" initial. |
| B Personal Portal | Arched doorway with a file stepping through | Reads as a bell, mailbox or padlock at small sizes; "files" meaning is lost | Rejected: ambiguous, and doorway metaphors suggest remote access, which the product deliberately is not. |
| C Connected Orbit | Tilted orbit around a folder, satellite dot | Strongest link to the name "Orbit", but the folder disappears and the shape reads as a planet below 32 px | Runner-up. Keep as the idea for illustrations and the "how it works" diagram, not the app icon. |

Honest weakness of A: a rounded M is a common shape, and it lacks the "orbit" idea. **No trademark or logo-similarity search has been done**; do that before approval.

## 2. Asset kit (`kit-provisional/`)
`svg/` masters (icon tile, mark light/dark, mono navy/white, horizontal and stacked lockups for light, dark and mono, wordmark, maskable and full-bleed icons); `png/` 16 to 512 px and lockups; `pwa/` 192, 512, maskable 512, apple-touch 180 and a manifest icon snippet; `favicon.ico` (16/32/48); `windows/` multi-size `.ico` (16 to 256) and tile/store PNGs using the same names as the shell extension assets; `social/og-image-1200x630.png`. The wordmark is outlined from Inter (SIL OFL), so no font is needed to render the SVGs. All generated by `tools/logos.py` and `tools/kit.py`.

## 3. Using the mark
- **Clear space:** half the mark's height on every side (`clear-space.png`).
- **Minimum size:** icon tile 16 px (favicon); horizontal lockup 24 px tall; stacked lockup 48 px tall. Below 24 px use the icon alone.
- **Backgrounds:** pearl or white: lockup light. Navy or teal: lockup dark or mono white. One colour only (print, embossing, tinted photos): mono navy or mono white.
- **Do not:** recolour the node, stretch, add shadows or outlines, rotate, put the light lockup on busy photos, rebuild the wordmark in another typeface.
- **Name:** "MyOrbitDrive", one word, capital M, O and D. Internal identifiers (namespaces, settings, repo) keep the old name by design.

## 4. Colour and type
Tokens are in `tokens.json` and `tokens.css`. Contrast, computed: navy on pearl 13.4:1, slate on pearl 7.7:1, teal on pearl 5.6:1 (passes AA for body text), white on teal 5.6:1, mint on navy 9.8:1. Mint is for highlights on navy only. Type is Inter 500/600/700, self-hosted later with a system-ui fallback (no third-party font requests, so no tracker exposure).

## 5. Voice
Plain, specific, calm. Say what the product does and what it does not do. Allowed claims are listed in `PRODUCT_AUDIT.md` section 3; never "end-to-end", "zero-knowledge", "guaranteed" or any comparison with a named competitor.
